Separated Measurement Guides

8 Parameter Measuring Protocols & Instructions

Follow each step exactly — the model expects measurements taken this way. Measure on the right side of your body with a non-stretch tape, to the nearest 0.1 cm (or 1/8 in). A helper makes every tape measurement more accurate.

01
Parameter 1

Age (Years)

Your age in completed years. At the same body size, body fat tends to change with age, so the model uses age alongside your measurements.

Enter your age in whole completed years - do not round up.
Use your age on the day you take the measurements.
02
Parameter 2

Biological Sex

At the same measurements, women on average carry more body fat than men, so the model needs to know.

Select the option that matches your biological sex.
03
Parameter 3

Body Weight (Mass)

Body weight on a digital floor scale, without shoes, wearing only light clothing.

Remove shoes and heavy clothing; use a hard, flat floor.
Stand still in the centre, hands at sides, and read once the display is stable.
For repeat checks, weigh at the same time of day.
04
Parameter 4

Standing Height

Maximum standing height, barefoot, against a wall or stadiometer.

Heels together, toes slightly out; heels, buttocks, shoulder blades and back of head touch the wall.
Look straight ahead with your head level, not tilted up or down.
Deep breath, stand tall; a helper presses a flat object onto the head and reads to 0.1 cm.
05
Parameter 5

Waist Circumference

Measured just above the top of the right hip bone - not at the belly button, which gives a different number.

Find the top of the right hip bone at the side of the body; the tape goes just above it.
Tape horizontal all the way round, snug but not pressing into the skin.
Read at the end of a normal breath out, without pulling the stomach in.
06
Parameter 6

Upper Arm Circumference

Right arm, halfway between the bony tip of the shoulder and the tip of the elbow - not the biggest part of the biceps.

Elbow bent 90°, palm up: measure shoulder tip to elbow tip down the back of the arm and mark halfway.
Let the arm hang loosely, muscles relaxed, and wrap the tape around the mark.
07
Parameter 7

Calf Circumference

The largest circumference of the right calf, measured sitting with the knee bent at 90°.

Sit with the right knee at a right angle and the leg relaxed.
Slide the tape up and down to find the widest point; keep it perpendicular to the lower leg.
08
Parameter 8

Thigh Circumference

Right thigh, halfway between the crease where the thigh meets the hip and the top edge of the kneecap.

Seated, knee 90°: measure crease to top of kneecap along the front of the thigh and mark halfway.
Stand with weight on the left leg, right knee slightly bent, and wrap the tape around the mark.
